## Learning through LULESH

The hands-on session introduced the fundamentals of HPC through **LULESH**—the Livermore Unstructured Lagrangian Explicit Shock Hydrodynamics mini-application developed by Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.

Participants worked on building and optimising LULESH while learning how computational work can be distributed and tuned to run more efficiently. The activity offered a practical look at the ideas behind scientific computing rather than treating HPC as a purely theoretical topic.
